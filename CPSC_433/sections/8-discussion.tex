\section{Discussion and Limitations}
The shared OR-tree stage may exhaust all alternatives without finding a feasible timetable. If it is interrupted before that determination, infeasibility has not been established. Set-based hill climbing can reach local minima because it only accepts improving Moves or Swaps. AND-tree branch-and-bound preserves a complete feasible incumbent while exploring the full assignment space, but its worst-case work is exponential; only a completed search or a zero lower bound with zero penalty establishes global optimality. For larger instances, a time budget may be needed, and the returned timetable should be labelled feasible rather than optimal when the search is interrupted.
