\section{Overall Search Architecture}
\subsection{How the models relate}
Our optional combined pipeline is
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\begin{gathered}
\text{OR-tree}\ \longrightarrow\ a_{\mathrm{feasible}}\\[-1pt]
\downarrow\\[-1pt]
\text{Set-based improvement}\ \longrightarrow\ a_{\mathrm{best}}\\[-1pt]
\downarrow\\[-1pt]
\text{AND-tree branch-and-bound, initialized with }a_{\mathrm{best}}.
\end{gathered}}
\end{equation}
The AND-tree explores the \emph{original scheduling choices}, not merely the local neighbors visited by set-based search. It can establish optimality only if its search completes with sound pruning.
